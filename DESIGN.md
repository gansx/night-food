# Night Food Design Direction

## Product Name

- Member web app: 家宴星球
- Owner admin app: 家宴中枢

## Visual Theme

Night Food should feel like a premium Web3 family cockpit, not a restaurant SaaS dashboard. The current visual reference is rock fused with polished glass: heavy, dark, reflective, and warm at the edges. Avoid bright cyber neon; use smoke-black surfaces, mineral blue reflections, mossy stone shadows, and amber-orange highlights.

## Color Roles

- Canvas: #020507
- Deep reflection: #082851
- Stone green: #295A44
- Glass teal: #22748D
- Heat accent: #E87748
- Warm highlight: #EF9F5D
- Sand text: #E7C9AE
- Panel: rgba(5, 11, 16, 0.78)
- Border: rgba(231, 201, 174, 0.16)

## Components

- Cards use translucent dark glass, soft neon borders, and large rounded corners.
- Primary actions use heat-orange to warm-sand gradients.
- Secondary actions use ghost buttons with subtle white borders.
- Navigation is desktop grid cards and mobile fixed bottom navigation.
- Touch targets should stay at least 44px high.

## Responsive Behavior

- Mobile layouts are not desktop pages scaled down.
- Main content collapses into single-column app-like cards.
- Primary navigation moves to a persistent bottom tab bar.
- Cards should remain readable at 320px width without horizontal scrolling.

## Guardrails

- Avoid beige, cafe, generic food delivery styling, and obvious neon green/cyan cyber palettes.
- Avoid decorative cards that do not navigate or expose useful data.
- Avoid huge paragraph hero copy; use a memorable product name and compact explanation.
- Preserve the family product story: menus, tasks, points, and household permissions.
